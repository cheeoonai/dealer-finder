# Cambodia

## General
- Outreach language: English works for most B2B; Khmer for local shops; Chinese for the many Chinese-run trading companies.
- Legal suffix: "Co., Ltd." Strip it when deduping.
- Phones: +855 (mobile 1x/6x/7x/8x/9x). Telegram and WhatsApp both common.
- Directories (Exa `--domains`): cambodiayp.com · contact.page (Cambodia Yellow Pages listings live on kh*.contact.page subdomains)
- Web presence is thin. Many dealers are only on Facebook or Google Maps — expect a short list.
- Many brands cover Cambodia from Thailand or Vietnam: also search `"<brand> distributor Thailand Cambodia"` / `"<brand> Indochina distributor"`.
- Chinese queries must include 柬埔寨 or 金边, or results drift to factories in China.
