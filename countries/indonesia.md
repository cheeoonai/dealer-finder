- Outreach language: Bahasa Indonesia, polite ("Bapak/Ibu").
- Legal suffixes: PT (company), CV (smaller partnership), Tbk (listed). PT + branches in several cities = bigger fish.
- Phones: WhatsApp +62 8…; landlines +62 21 Jakarta, +62 31 Surabaya, +62 778 Batam.
- Directories (Exa `--domains`): indotrading.com · indonetwork.co.id · tokopedia.com · ralali.com · monotaro.id
